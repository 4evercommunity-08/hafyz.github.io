About

The Artist

HAFYZ is a multi-disciplinary musician, composer, producer, and entrepreneur from Pekanbaru, Indonesia. Known for fluid and characterful sonic exploration, he consistently blurs genre boundaries.

The Journey

Starting in 2015 with an audio-visual production house, HAFYZ officially entered the music industry with debut single "Paradise" in 2017. As a visionary creator, he controls every blueprint of his work — executive producer and recording engineer.

Discography

From MANIC (2020) to Immortal Romance (Extended Deluxe Versions) (2025), and the sixth album xoxo, i'm done (2026). Each release is an experiment in modern narrative.

SOUR+ Corporation

Beyond the studio, HAFYZ translates his artistic vision into a broader professional realm under SOUR+ Corporation — integrating music (SOUR+ Entertainment Inc.) and cinema (Balai Kreasi Cinema).
