Artist

The artist.

A concise professional profile for media, industry, brand and event partners.

HAFYZ is the stage name of Muhammad Hafiz — a musician, composer and producer from Pekanbaru, Indonesia, who builds seasons: each release a new chapter of one ongoing story, moving fluidly across Electronic, Indie and Pop.

He began in 2015 with an audio-visual production house and debuted with the single "Paradise" in 2017. His catalogue has since grown into a series of connected chapters — HAFIZ (Apple Music Edition) (2023), Immortal Romance (2024) and the instrumental EP love letters i'll never sent (2025) — and he remains hands-on across the creative and production process of his work.

His sixth studio album, xoxo, i'm done, opened the After Hours era in 2026; its 19-track deluxe edition followed on October 23, 2026. From tropical Pekanbaru he then wrote his first winter: the holiday single "mistletoe" (November 13, 2026) and his first holiday EP, December, with you (December 4, 2026), ahead of a new chapter in January 2027. His work extends into visual culture, cinema and creative entrepreneurship within the broader ecosystem of SOUR+ Corporation, including SOUR+ Entertainment Inc. and Balai Kreasi Cinema, and he is a member of The Ivors Academy.

For selected events, HAFYZ may participate as an invited guest, public-facing creative professional, media guest, panel participant, presenter or industry attendee, depending on the nature and requirements of the event.
