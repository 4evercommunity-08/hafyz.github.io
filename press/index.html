The Artist

HAFYZ

HAFYZ is a multi-disciplinary musician, composer, producer, and entrepreneur from Pekanbaru, Indonesia. Known for fluid and characterful sonic exploration, he consistently blurs genre boundaries across Electronic, Indie, and Pop.

Starting in 2015 with an audio-visual production house, HAFYZ officially entered the music industry with debut single "Paradise" in 2017. As a visionary creator, he controls every blueprint of his work — serving as executive producer and recording engineer on all releases.

From MANIC (2020) to Immortal Romance (Extended Deluxe Versions) (2025), and the sixth album xoxo, i'm done (2026), each release is an experiment in modern narrative. Beyond the studio, HAFYZ translates his artistic vision into a broader professional realm under SOUR+ Corporation — integrating music (SOUR+ Entertainment Inc.) and cinema (Balai Kreasi Cinema).

His latest single "chasing hollow gold (After Hours)" — released July 31, 2026 — marks the official opening of the After Hours era, exploring themes of friendship breakup with emotional depth and sonic precision.
