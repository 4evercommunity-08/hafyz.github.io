About

Beyond the track.

HAFYZ is a musician, composer, producer and entrepreneur from Pekanbaru. He writes songs about boundaries, leftover versions of yourself, and the mess that still wants to stay.

The journey

He started in 2015 with an audio-visual production house, then entered music with the debut single “Paradise” in 2017. He still produces and engineers his own records.
