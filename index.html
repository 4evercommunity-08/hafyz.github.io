About

Beyond the track.

HAFYZ is the stage name of Muhammad Hafiz — a musician, composer, and producer from Pekanbaru, Indonesia, who doesn't just release songs, but builds seasons: each release a new chapter of one ongoing story.

The journey

HAFYZ entered the music industry with his debut single “Paradise” in 2017, after starting out in 2015 with an audio-visual production house. Since then, he has grown a catalogue written in series rather than singles — including the instrumental album “HAFIZ (Apple Music Edition)” (2023), “Immortal Romance” (2024), and the instrumental EP “love letters i'll never sent” (2025).

Discography

His sixth studio album, xoxo, i'm done, opened the After Hours era in 2026.

The album later expanded into a 19-track deluxe edition, “xoxo, i'm done (After Hours Deluxe)”. That same year, from tropical Pekanbaru, he wrote his first winter: “mistletoe”, his first holiday single, leading into his first holiday EP, “December, with you” — with a new chapter arriving in January 2027.

SOUR+ Corporation

His professional ecosystem extends through SOUR+ Corporation, including SOUR+ Entertainment Inc. and Balai Kreasi Cinema.
