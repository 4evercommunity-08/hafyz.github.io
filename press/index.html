Artist

The artist.

A concise professional overview for media, industry and event partners.

HAFYZ is the stage name of Muhammad Hafiz — a musician, composer, and producer from Pekanbaru, Indonesia. He releases music the way stories unfold: not as standalone songs, but as seasons, each release a new chapter of one ongoing narrative across Electronic, Indie, and Pop.

HAFYZ began in 2015 with an audio-visual production house before officially entering the music industry with his debut single "Paradise" in 2017. He has since built a catalogue written in series rather than singles — including the instrumental album HAFIZ (Apple Music Edition) (2023), Immortal Romance (2024), and the instrumental EP love letters i'll never sent (2025) — serving as executive producer and recording engineer on all releases.

In 2026, his sixth studio album xoxo, i'm done opened the After Hours era — introduced by the single "Chasing Hollow Gold" (July 31, 2026) and expanded on October 23, 2026 into xoxo, i'm done (After Hours Deluxe), a 19-track edition. In November 2026, "THIS IS FOR YOU (After Hours)" was placed on the Weekly Dose of Pop and Asian Topping Marmalade playlists. Beyond the studio, HAFYZ works under SOUR+ Entertainment Inc., part of SOUR+ Corporation, and is a member of The Ivors Academy.

On November 13, 2026, HAFYZ releases "mistletoe", his first holiday single — written from tropical Pekanbaru — as the lead single of his first holiday EP, December, with you (December 4, 2026). A new chapter follows in January 2027, with a new single on January 8 and a new EP on January 29.
