01 / Artist

The artist.

A concise professional profile for media, industry, brand and event partners.

HAFYZ

HAFYZ is a multi-disciplinary musician, composer, producer, and entrepreneur from Pekanbaru, Indonesia. Known for fluid and characterful sonic exploration, he consistently blurs genre boundaries across Electronic, Indie, and Pop.

Starting in 2015 with an audio-visual production house, HAFYZ officially entered the music industry with his debut single "Paradise" in 2017. As a multidisciplinary creator, he remains involved across the creative and production process of his work.

His work extends beyond recorded music into visual culture, cinema, creative entrepreneurship and the broader professional ecosystem of SOUR+ Corporation, including music through SOUR+ Entertainment Inc. and cinema through Balai Kreasi Cinema.

For selected events, HAFYZ may participate as an invited guest, public-facing creative professional, media guest, panel participant, presenter or industry attendee, depending on the nature and requirements of the event.
