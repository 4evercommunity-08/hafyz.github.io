About

Beyond the track.

HAFYZ is a multi-disciplinary musician, composer, producer and entrepreneur from Pekanbaru, Indonesia, known for fluid sonic exploration and genre-blurring work.

The journey

Starting in 2015 with an audio-visual production house, HAFYZ officially entered the music industry with debut single “Paradise” in 2017.

Discography

From MANIC to Immortal Romance and the sixth album xoxo, i'm done, each release explores a different piece of the larger narrative.

SOUR+ Corporation

His professional ecosystem extends through SOUR+ Corporation, including SOUR+ Entertainment Inc. and Balai Kreasi Cinema.
