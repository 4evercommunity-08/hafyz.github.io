About the Song

chasing hollow gold (after hours) serves as the third single released from HAFYZ's After Hours era. Sonically driven by a heavy and gritty dark alt-pop landscape, the track delivers a raw, uncompromising, and completely unfiltered explicit narrative.

The core concept of the song stems from a deeply bitter personal reflection on a devastating friendship and professional breakup that completely dismantled HAFYZ's inner circle. The title itself acts as a sharp metaphor for superficial relationships built on one-sided gains — connections that appear shiny, grand, and valuable like gold on the outside, but are entirely empty (hollow) and toxic within.

Through this intense cathartic release, HAFYZ channels the absolute exhaustion of being exploited, turning the trauma of betrayal into a bold sonic statement where he chooses to strike back with subtle psychological shade rather than staying silent.

The hype surrounding the track was instantly ignited during its initial rollout, sparked by a cryptic acrostic code embedded within the official announcement on the SOUR+ Entertainment label Instagram account. By isolating the oddly capitalized words to uncover the A-G-H-Z initials, fans were immediately drawn into decoding the hidden messages woven into the track's very fabric.

By combining raw frustration, a dark sonic atmosphere, and a real-world falling out, this single stands out as one of the most daring and emotionally charged releases in HAFYZ's discography. Slated for an official release on July 31, 2026, the track promises an unflinchingly honest and powerful emotional impact, marking a pivotal moment where HAFYZ completely reclaims his narrative.
