xoxo, i’m done (After Hours) is a 13-track album about looking at different sides of yourself after everything has happened. It explores the space between who you were, who you've become, and the parts of you that never really left.

The album

After Hours
lets them exist.

Some songs are reflective and grounded, while others are impulsive, playful, messy, and unapologetic.

At its core, the album is about growing without pretending that growth means leaving every part of your past behind. There is the version of HAFYZ that has learned to set boundaries, understand what needs to be left behind, and move forward. But there is also the younger, more restless side that still wants to react, run away, laugh at the chaos, or simply say, “fuck it.”

Rather than choosing between those two sides, After Hours lets them exist together.
